# Week 10, Day 5 -Let's Build Our First AI Agent

*November 1, 2026 · [Originally published on Substack](https://niharikabatra.substack.com/p/week-10-day-5-lets-build-our-first)*

Yesterday, we explored how an AI agent makes decisions and remembers information.

Today, let’s build something!

Imagine opening your inbox on a Monday morning.

You receive an email:

> Hi,
> 
> I wanted to check whether you’ve completed the project report. We need it before Friday’s meeting.
> 
> Thanks!

You want to reply, but before writing anything, you need to understand what the sender is asking, decide what information to include, and make sure your response is accurate.

What if an AI assistant could help with all of this?

That’s what we’re going to build.
