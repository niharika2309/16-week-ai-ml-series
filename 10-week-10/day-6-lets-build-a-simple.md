# Week 10, Day 6 - How Does an Autonomous AI Agent Work?

*November 2, 2026 · [Originally published on Substack](https://niharikabatra.substack.com/p/week-10-day-6-lets-build-a-simple)*

Yesterday, we explored how multiple LLM calls can work together to complete a task.

But what if we stopped deciding every step in advance?

What if we gave an AI system a goal and allowed it to decide what to do next?

That’s where autonomous agents come in.

Today, we’re revisiting the same customer email example from earlier this week. But instead of following a predefined workflow, we’ll explore how an agent can work toward the goal on its own.
