We have spent the last few weeks exploring how AI retrieves information, generates answers, and evaluates whether those answers are good enough.

But what happens when answering a question is not enough?

Imagine asking an AI assistant to help you plan a weekend trip.

It can suggest destinations, recommend places to visit, and create an itinerary. That’s useful.

Now imagine giving it a goal: plan a two-day trip within a $500 budget. It researches destinations, compares hotel prices, checks travel costs, adjusts its recommendations when something exceeds the budget, and prepares a final plan.

**The difference is that the second system is working toward a goal rather than simply responding to individual questions.**

This is where AI agents come in.
