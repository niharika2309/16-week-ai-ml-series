Yesterday, we explored Guardrails in AI and how they help control what an AI application can and cannot do.

But there is another problem that guardrails alone cannot solve.

Imagine asking an AI assistant:

*“What is our company’s maternity leave policy?”*

The AI responds confidently:

*“Employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.”*

It sounds convincing. The answer is well-written, specific, and delivered with confidence.

But what if your company’s actual policy offers only 12 weeks?

The AI didn’t necessarily misunderstand your question. It generated an answer that sounded reasonable but wasn’t factually correct.

> **This is called an AI hallucination.**

Let’s understand why this happens and, more importantly, how we can reduce it.
