# Week 9, Day 2 - LLM-as-a-Judge : How Do We Use AI to Evaluate AI?

*October 22, 2026 · [Originally published on Substack](https://niharikabatra.substack.com/p/week-9-day-2-llm-as-a-judge-how-do)*

Yesterday, we explored why evaluating a RAG application matters. Today, let’s take the next step: What if we could use an LLM to evaluate the answers generated by our RAG application?

Imagine your RAG application answers thousands of customer questions every day. How would you know whether its answers are correct, relevant, and supported by the documents it retrieves?

Manually reviewing every answer would be time-consuming. Traditional evaluation methods can help, but comparing answers word for word isn’t always useful because two answers can communicate the same meaning using different words.

This is where **LLM-as-a-Judge comes in.**

---
### 1\. What is LLM-as-a-Judge?

LLM-as-a-Judge is an evaluation technique in which we use an LLM to assess the quality of an answer generated by another LLM.
