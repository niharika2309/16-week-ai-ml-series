# Week 11, Day 1 - Agent Harness : It's Not as Complicated as It Sounds

*November 4, 2026 · [Originally published on Substack](https://niharikabatra.substack.com/p/week-11-day-1-agent-harness-its-not)*

Last week, we explored how AI agents work.

This week, we’re going one level deeper.

And don’t worry. The word harness sounds much more complicated than the concept itself.

## First, a little AI news

Have you noticed that companies are talking more about how to build AI agents, not just how to make their models smarter?

In February 2026, OpenAI published an article titled Harness Engineering: Leveraging Codex in an Agent-First World.

> **The idea is fascinating: instead of manually writing every line of code, engineers can focus on creating the environment, instructions, tools, and feedback mechanisms that allow AI agents to do the work.**

And this growing focus on harness engineering is a signal that building reliable AI agents involves much more than choosing a powerful LLM.

---

## So, what is an agent harness?

Imagine asking an AI agent to find an email and draft a reply.

The LLM can decide what needs to happen.

But it also needs a way to access your emails, execute actions, receive results, and continue working.

> The software that makes all of this possible is called an **agent harness.**

Think of it as the setup that allows an LLM to operate as an agent.

## Why should we care?

Because even a very capable LLM can struggle when it doesn’t have the right tools, context, or execution environment.

**A harness helps bridge that gap.**

And here’s the exciting part: we can build one ourselves.

Over the next few days, we’ll explore the components of an agent harness, understand what each one does, and gradually build our own.

No need to be intimidated by the terminology.

We’re going to break it down, one component at a time.
