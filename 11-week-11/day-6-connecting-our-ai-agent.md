Over the past few days, we’ve explored agent harnesses, understood MCP, built our own MCP server, and discussed how MCP differs from directly connecting to APIs.

But we’ve mostly looked at these concepts individually. Today, let’s bring them together!

What happens when we give an AI agent access to the tools exposed by an MCP server? Can it discover those tools, decide which ones to use, and complete a task on its own?

Let’s find out.
