Yesterday, we explored how MCP helps an AI assistant connect to external tools.

Today, let’s take that idea one step further.

Instead of just understanding how MCP works, let’s look at how we can build our own MCP server.

And rather than creating another weather app or calculator, I wanted to build something I could actually use.

**I have been publishing a 16-week AI/ML series on Substack and maintaining a companion curriculum on GitHub.**

One thing I often have to do is manually move published articles from Substack into my GitHub repository.

So I thought: Why not build an MCP server that connects the two?

Let’s understand what we’re building and, more importantly, how MCP makes it possible.
